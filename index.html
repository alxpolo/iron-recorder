Recorder Inbox
Connect your recorder, then tap Import recordings. On iPhone, choose the audio files from the external drive in Files.

Free/local mode uses Whisper Tiny in your browser. The first transcription downloads the speech model, so the first run can take longer and use significant data. After that, the browser may cache it. Your original audio stays on-device in this app's browser storage unless you export it yourself.
